> An authentic virtual Pakhawaj and Tabla riyaz companion designed for classical Indian music practitioners, Dhrupad vocalists, and instrumentalists.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **High-Fidelity Acoustic Samples:** Recorded in high definition from authentic handmade classical Pakhawaj instruments.
- **Comprehensive Taals:** Built-in Dhrupad & Haveli Sangeet taals including Chautaal (12 beats), Dhamar (14 beats), Sooltaal (10 beats), Teevra (7 beats), and Roopak.
- **Zero-Latency Sound Engine:** Low-latency playback buffer ensuring steady rhythmic precision (Laya) during live riyaz.
- **Pitch (Sur) & Tempo (BPM) Adjustment:** Fine-tuned microtonal scale tuning (Sa from A to G#) and wide BPM controls (Vilambit, Madhaya, Drut).
- **Offline Riyaz Mode:** Fully functional without an active internet connection.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
